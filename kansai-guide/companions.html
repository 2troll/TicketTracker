Ideas · What's Possible

A Day With Your Guide

There is no script, no fixed route and no group. You tell your guide what you want — or you ask them to surprise you. Here are some of the things people choose.

🍜
The Ramen Counter Nobody Knows
A tiny 8-seat counter in an Osaka alley. No sign outside, no English menu. Tony orders for you in Japanese. Best bowl you will eat in Japan.
🏔
Summit Kongō Before Sunrise
Take the train to the trailhead in the dark. Walk the cedar path by headlamp. Stand 1,125m above Osaka as the city lights slowly dim below. Yours alone.
⛩
Fushimi Inari at 5am
10,000 torii gates, morning mist and nobody there. We take the first train. Most visitors arrive after 9am. You will be gone by then.
🎴
Pottery Workshop with a Master
A traditional Kyoto studio. Throw your own piece, have it fired and shipped home. Johnny arranges everything, including the translation.
🛁
Neighbourhood Sento at Dusk
A local public bath in Osaka. Tiles from the 1950s, regulars who have been coming for 40 years. The most Japanese hour of your entire trip.
🍶
Sake Brewery in Nada, Kobe
Japan's oldest sake district, 20 minutes by train from Osaka. Private tour of a working brewery, blind tasting, barrel room. Larion translates with the brewmaster directly.
🌸
Cherry Blossom Spots Off the Map
Not Maruyama Park. Tony has five locations he has never published anywhere — canal paths, mountain clearings, a hidden temple garden. One visit each spring.
🎭
Nishiki Market — Every Stall
Kyoto's 400-year-old covered food market. Most visitors walk through in 20 minutes. With Larion you spend two hours — tasting, talking, learning exactly what you are eating.
🌃
Osaka at Night from Above
A rooftop viewpoint Tony has used for years. Not Umeda Sky Building. A quieter spot, no tickets, Osaka Bay to the south and Kyoto's mountains to the north.
🎸
Underground Jazz Bar Crawl
Osaka has a remarkable jazz scene. Basement bars that don't appear on Google Maps, run by musicians, open until 3am. Johnny takes you in as a regular.
🏯
Private Temple Access at Dawn
Some Kyoto temples open early for meditation visitors. Arrive before the gates open to the public. Stand in a 1,000-year-old garden alone, in silence.
🚂
Any Train, Any Direction
You point at the map and say "there." We go. I buy the tickets, navigate the transfers, explain what we pass. Kansai by train is the best way to see Japan.

All travel is by train. We meet you at your nearest station and travel together through Kansai — no private car, no driver. Just you, your guide, and the best train network in the world.
