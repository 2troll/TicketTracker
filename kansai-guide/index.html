The Region · 関西地方

Where Japan's history was born

Kansai is Japan's cultural and spiritual core. Home to two ancient capitals, the country's finest cuisine, and a way of life that remains fiercely distinct — warmer, louder, more honest. Seven prefectures. Centuries of stories. Knowing where to look makes all the difference.

大阪

Osaka

Japan's kitchen. Street food, neon, merchant spirit, and the most open-hearted people you'll meet anywhere in the country.

京都

Kyoto

A thousand years of imperial culture. Wooden temples, bamboo groves, geisha districts — and the hidden paths tourists rarely find.

奈良

Nara

Ancient deer roam freely among UNESCO shrines. Japan's first permanent capital — serene, underrated, unforgettable.

神戸

Kobe

Port city elegance, legendary beef, and a European-influenced skyline nestled between mountains and sea.

姫路

Himeji

Japan's most perfect castle — white as a heron in flight, unchanged for four centuries.

高野山

Kōyasan

Sleep in a mountain monastery. Walk lantern-lit cemetery paths at dawn. A world completely apart.

Meet Tony


I'm Tony — a private guide based in the Kansai region. I came to Japan drawn by its culture, its food, and its people, and I never left.

Questions & Answers

Everything You Need to Know

Not a single word. I handle all communication — with restaurants, vendors, temple staff and locals. You just enjoy Japan. All tours are available in English, Spanish, Arabic, Russian and Czech.
Cash in Japanese Yen on the day, or bank transfer before the tour. No card machines needed — I'll confirm all details by WhatsApp or email beforehand so there are no surprises.
Free cancellation up to 48 hours before. Within 48–24 hours: 50% applies. Within 24 hours: full fee. If I need to cancel for any reason, you receive a 100% refund. Rescheduling is always free.
Yes. The city tours are completely adaptable — pace, distance and activities are built around your group. Hiking tours have three difficulty levels. Families with young children regularly join the city and cultural tours.
Comfortable walking shoes (trail shoes for harder routes), layers (Japanese mountains change fast), and a small daypack with water. I provide snacks, a packed Japanese bento lunch, first-aid kit and all trail permits.
Light rain doesn't stop us — Japan is beautiful in the rain and most routes are fine. For heavy rain or typhoon warnings, we reschedule at no charge and find the best alternative. I monitor forecasts and contact you in advance.
Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, halal, gluten-free — Japan has more options than most people expect. Just tell me beforehand and I'll plan the food stops and bento accordingly. I've guided guests with every kind of dietary requirement.
For peak seasons (cherry blossom in April, autumn leaves in November) I recommend booking 4–6 weeks ahead. For other times, 1–2 weeks is usually fine. Last-minute requests sometimes work — just ask on WhatsApp.
