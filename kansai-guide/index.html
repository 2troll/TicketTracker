Meet Tony

I'm Tony — a private guide based in Osaka. I came to Japan drawn by its culture, food, and people — and I never left.

What I offer is not a packaged tour. It is access to the Japan I know personally — the backstreet izakayas not on Google Maps, the temple at 6am before the crowds, the market vendor who becomes your favourite memory.

I speak English, Spanish, and Arabic fluently, and adapt to any pace or interest — whether you want to eat everything, photograph every corner, or simply walk and let the city breathe around you.
