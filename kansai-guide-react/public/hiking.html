The Six Routes

From Osaka's snow-capped crown to Japan's oldest pilgrimage road — these are the mountains that define Kansai. Climbed in every season. Guided for every level.

1,125 m · Osaka Prefecture · Tony's #1 Summit
Mt. Kongō金剛山 · Osaka's Crown

Osaka's highest peak and its most rewarding. Dense cedar columns rising to a frost-covered summit that turns white in January — impossible to believe is 45 minutes from Namba. The trail through Chihayafuru is silent, ancient and perfect. I have climbed this mountain in every season and it is never the same twice.
